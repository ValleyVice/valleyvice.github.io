If I Never Sleep I Never Rest

My head... - 7/14

I was sore from last night it was the best
I worked a full shift
and couldn't sleep after because my head
If I never sleep I never rest.

I still feel my from when we meshed
We danced all night and couldn't find a misstep
But we couldnt sleep after because my head
if I never sleep I never rest.

My father called and said we were blessed
My lover called and noted we should have sex
My body killed itself and ate its own heart
and I could finally sleep after everyone left.

End.
