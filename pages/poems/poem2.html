Poetry

Cold Alone

How Brood. - 7/12

we let the wind in
nothing ever felt like this
cold alone,

it's not that dark, the windows are blacked out
Im shivering so bad, i wish you were here
I was so late,
I felt crushed,
I saw, i, fall victim to the glorification of my partner and our time

im so cold and high,
im so high
i dont wanna see the light at five
because thats when i find sleep
and your wake up time.

End.
