Poetry

Originality, and the Valley

Yum- 7/21

Working in the service industry, you see the pinnacle of human originality.
You see 3 Number Fours and a couple orders of the same thing. But in the
Valley, I'm tired of fast food. Less and less people coming, thinking,
Appreciating what they put into themselves. That's the art in the Valley.
When no one even wants to order anything new; no one wants to make any-
thing new. It feels like a genuine cesspool. I feel like everyone's
waiting for a new Big Mac or a Jumbo Jack, especially when there's
better or different options. Everyone's so desperate for taste, a
type of taste, then go for the usual. The go to, the same outfit,
the same order, the same size, the same fries, then we suffer for it.
We all fall into complacency sooo easily, I'm victim.
I hear so much: "we dont have time for that", "I don't know how to
make that", "it's too much".

Okay, don't tell me it'll ever taste as good as when you make it.

End.
