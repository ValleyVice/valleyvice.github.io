Sober To Thursday

And thats today... - 7/16

Today is the best day of my life,
my american spirit is my best high,
the land of the love that crushes me,
camel blue crushes the booze in me,
here's one way we keep the stupidity:
if we drink and smoke like we never
loved anything

You say the things i am is lovely,
ridiculous to think anyone would love me,
but today is thursday, and ill get high,
liqour and leaf like we all say this time,
im sober to thursday,
but this week feels like ive been sober my whole life.

End.
